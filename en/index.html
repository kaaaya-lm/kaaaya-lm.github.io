LM Strategy Simulator Lords Mobile Calculator App

A free mobile app (not a web tool) for Lords Mobile players. Install it to access 9 calculation tools: battle prediction, gear optimization, artifact efficiency, and more. Available for iPhone and Android.

iOS (App Store) Android (APK Download)

9 Calculation Tools for Lords Mobile Strategy

LM Strategy Simulator is a free app that brings calculation tools for Lords Mobile together in one place. Just enter your numbers to calculate the following.

Main Tools
Battle Simulator

Enter boosts and troop composition to quickly estimate battle outcomes.

Equipment Simulator

Find the best equipment combination, including Jewels and Sigils, and see upgrade efficiency.

Artifact Efficiency Table

Calculates level-up and upgrade efficiency so you can see the most efficient training priorities.

Castle Skin Efficiency Table

Calculates Castle Skin upgrade efficiency so you can pick the most cost-effective upgrades.

Training Simulator

Calculates the resources and speedups needed to train efficiently.

Other Useful Tools
Rally Report Calculator

Estimates the enemy's troop tier losses from the rally report numbers.

Troop Ratio Calculator

Enter troop type ratios, tier ratios, and army size to automatically calculate the troop breakdown.

Anti Troops Calculator

Estimates a defending castle's garrison from the reported kill increases.

Front Check

Determines a target castle's vanguard troop type from the power drop of three attack patterns.

The Equipment, Artifact, and Battle screens also support auto-fill from screenshots. Works on iPhone and Android.
